# Selection and evidence

Checked 7 November 2026. Family: litigation-fact-management. The registry had no build for this family when Casefleet was selected. Existing legal practice, discovery review and investigation builds do different jobs. This build focuses on human-authored facts, dated chronologies, pinpoint citations, issues and witness preparation.

## Annual bill first

US$25,200 a year is a scenario for fifteen Advanced seats at US$140 per user per month on annual billing. It is not a customer invoice or a measured average. The vendor quotes Enterprise for twenty or more users, so extending the public per-user rate to that tier would be misleading. Public page copy uses only the per-user rates and their annual equivalent, not an assumed firm bill.

Source: [Casefleet pricing](https://www.casefleet.com/pricing). The same page's structured offers identify annual and month-to-month billing. Starter is US$30 per user per month on annual billing; Advanced is US$140 annually billed or US$180 monthly billed. Advanced includes 5 GB per account before US$10 per GB per month overage. Prices are USD, not NZD or AUD.

## Export and product scope

[Casefleet reports](https://support.casefleet.com/en/articles/2013230-exporting-facts-and-running-reports) documents the Excel Fact Spreadsheet and source exports. [Account export guidance](https://support.casefleet.com/en/articles/1944257-exporting-your-data) distinguishes raw account data from original source documents.

The exporter documentation describes content, not exact workbook headers. We tested synthetic CSV and XLSX files, date cells, header mapping, duplicate rows, rejected dates and rollback. An actual authenticated Casefleet export was not available. The importer preserves all original fields but does not infer evidence relationships from source descriptions. There is no claim of full service parity, automatic legal analysis or court filing.

## Comparison honesty

Casefleet already supports fact filtering, reports, chronology and evidence links. We do not claim it cannot answer a question merely because this build can. The differentiated offer is an owned database, code and custom review rules with Enterprise DNA operating the agreed version. Document viewing, transcription and multi-user access require separately scoped work.
